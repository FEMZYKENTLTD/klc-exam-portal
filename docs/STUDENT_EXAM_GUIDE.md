# KLC CBT Suite v1.0 — Student Exam Guide

## Before the exam
- Register once (mandatory — no registration, no exam). Your Result PIN is
  shown at registration: `SURNAME+CLASS` (e.g. `KERIPESS2`).
- Check your passport photo appears on the exam instructions screen — that
  is your identity check.

## Taking the exam
1. Login → select **Subject** → Start Exam → read instructions → accept.
2. The exam opens **full-screen**. Do NOT minimize or Alt-Tab:
   - Strike 1 = warning · Strike 2 = final warning · **Strike 3 = exam is
     submitted automatically and your account is locked 15 minutes**.
3. Use the **Question Navigator** (1–60) — green = answered, orange = flagged.
4. Flag any question for review with **F**. Navigate with **N / P** or the
   arrow keys. Pick options with keys **1–5 (A–E)**.
5. **Calculator** (basic + scientific toggle) and **Formula Sheet** are in
   the toolbar. Audio questions have a **🔊 Play Audio** button.
6. Accessibility: **A-/A+** font, **◐ high contrast**, dyslexic font.
7. Your answers **save automatically every 30 seconds** — a power cut loses
   nothing. Timer warnings sound at 15 and 5 minutes; auto-submit at zero.

## After the exam
- Instant score + topic breakdown. Full history lives in **My Results**
  (report card, transcript downloads). Parents use the web checker with your
  Admission No + Result PIN.
- Practice mode = unlimited attempts, never recorded.
